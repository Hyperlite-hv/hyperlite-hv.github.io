
### Fixed

- The dashboard reads as one product in French: the predefined roles (Reader, Operator, Manager, Backup store reader)
  and their descriptions, why automatic HA cannot restart, and why a VM's console needs a restart were shown in English
  as the API wrote them.
- Forms keep their rhythm: the cluster page's create form no longer spreads its fields to match the join form, and a
  text area shows the rows it asks for (the join information folded into one line); a list of a few choices no longer
  stretches across a wide card.
- The nodes list fits a laptop screen: the local node says "Local node" once, and a member's address goes under it;
  every column, the uptime included, shows at 1440 px.
- A VM's summary writes a missing value as text, never in monospace; its figures line up at the top whether or not
  they have a second line; its backup action stands out only while nothing is scheduled.
- Warnings, errors and pending changes share one look, a tinted border all round instead of a thick bar on the left;
  on a phone only the page's primary action takes the whole width; the console's modes wrap whole instead of folding
  their labels; checkboxes, the text caret and the selection follow the theme; a figure and its unit (8 Go, 3 Ko/s,
  5 h 24 min) never part at the end of a line.

- The dashboard fits more screens: a table row on two lines (a node and its role) no longer touches the card's
  bottom; on a very wide screen the pages keep a readable width, centred; on a phone the overview's node table drops
  its optional columns and bars instead of scrolling inside its card; a node's band in the VM list no longer runs
  24 px past the table; the VM list's column picker opens towards the side that has room, never off the screen.
